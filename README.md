# ST3188 Market Research Proposal – Marriott International

## Project Overview

This project was completed for the ST3188 Statistical Methods for Market Research module.  
The task required developing a full professional research proposal in response to a real-world client brief from Marriott International.

The objective was to design a statistically rigorous market research plan that addresses the client’s business objectives and research aims.

---

# Client Context

Marriott International aims to:

- Expand global presence and brand diversity
- Improve personalised customer experiences
- Evaluate loyalty programme effectiveness
- Assess sustainability impact on customer loyalty
- Understand emerging travel trends and demographic differences

The research proposal was developed to align statistical methodology with these strategic objectives.

---

# Technical Requirements

The coursework required:

- Interpreting and summarising the client brief
- Translating business objectives into measurable research aims
- Designing a justified fieldwork strategy
- Proposing appropriate sampling methodology
- Determining suitable sample sizes using statistical principles
- Designing a structured questionnaire
- Proposing suitable multivariate statistical techniques
- Explaining how the analysis would support business decisions

No primary data collection or actual modelling was required — only a fully justified research design.

---

# Research Design Components

## Fieldwork Design

- Online survey methodology (with justification)
- Consideration of alternative modes (e.g. mixed-mode, telephone)
- Inclusion of both Marriott and competitor customers

## Sampling Strategy

- Use of Marriott customer database as sampling frame
- Justified probability-based sampling approach
- Segmentation across geography and customer types
- Statistical reasoning for required sample sizes
- Confidence interval considerations

## Data to be Collected

- Customer preferences for technology and personalisation
- Satisfaction measures across brands and regions
- Loyalty programme perceptions
- Sustainability attitudes
- Travel behaviour and economic sensitivity
- Demographic segmentation variables

---

# Proposed Statistical Techniques

The proposal justified the use of:

- Analysis of Variance (ANOVA)
- Multiple Regression
- Factor Analysis
- Cluster Analysis

Each technique was linked directly to specific research aims, demonstrating how statistical modelling would generate actionable business insights.

---

# Deliverables

The final submission included:

- Executive summary
- Full research proposal (≤ 3,000 words)
- Sampling justification and confidence interval logic
- Questionnaire design
- References
